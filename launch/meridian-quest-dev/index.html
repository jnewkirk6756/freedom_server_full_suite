GET STARTED

Measure in passthrough AR

  1. Open this link in Meta Quest Browser and choose Enter passthrough AR.
  2. Select Measure on the floating panel. Aim at a detected surface dot, then press the trigger for A and B.
  3. If there is no surface dot, choose Controller position explicitly. Place the tracked controller origin at each safely reachable point.
  4. Use Tools & area for endpoint snap, undo and two perpendicular sides of a rectangle. Squeeze the grip to recenter the panel.

Stay stationary in a clear space. Measurements are approximate. Verify with a tape or laser before estimates or cutting. Tracking interruptions and ending XR clear measurements.
